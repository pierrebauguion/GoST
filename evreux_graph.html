Evreux warehouse network

Layout computed from walking costs (MDS + stress majorization): close nodes are close in travel cost, the picture is not the exact floor plan. Hover a node for its id. Red: aisles of the pick list; black: depot (cart zone, node 223). The depot merges several cart points and is linked to most aisles: it is drawn near the aisles it reaches most cheaply, and its arcs are hidden by default. Arcs are drawn without direction.
